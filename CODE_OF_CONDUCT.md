# Code of Conduct

This project serves community associations — volunteers, board members, and the
developers who help them. We hold contributors to the same standard we would expect in
any of those associations: respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good faith. Review the code, not the person.
- Give and accept feedback with technical rigor and without personal remarks.
- Respect differing viewpoints and the time of volunteers.
- Keep discussions on topic and in a language everyone in the thread can follow
  (issues and pull requests are in English or Portuguese).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information (contact details, photos, member data).
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any channel where someone represents the project. Reports go to
**[bruno@bolalabs.pt](mailto:bruno@bolalabs.pt)** and are handled confidentially. The
maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.
